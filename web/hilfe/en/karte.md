# The map

By default the map shows the swisstopo aerial image. Pan and zoom as usual: drag, two fingers or mouse wheel.

## Map buttons {#knoepfe}

The map buttons are at the bottom right. Red numbers show unread or open items (casualties not rescued, unread messages), the neutral number on “Responders” the number of participants.

| Button | Opens |
|---|---|
| Camera focus | Choice of what the map should show (see below) |
| Responders | List of all responders, grouped by special role ([more](#einsatz/einsatzkraefte)) |
| Casualties | Missing and found persons ([more](#einsatz/rettlinge)) |
| Messages | Messages of the operation ([more](#einsatz/nachrichten)) |
| Radio | Announcements and live conference ([more](#einsatz/funk)) |
| Markers | PLS, markers, areas, lines and fixed locations ([more](#einsatz/markierungen)) |
| Gauging stations | Water level and discharge ([more](#einsatz/messstationen)) |
| River distance | Distance between two points along the water ([more](#einsatz/flussdistanz)) |

The **»** button above the map buttons folds them away so that more map is visible on a phone. Folded, it shows the sum of the red numbers. On a phone the side windows cover the map buttons; they close via ✕.

## View {#ansicht}

The button at the top right (layers icon) opens the **View** window:

- **Base map**: aerial images and maps that your organisation has switched on. The choice applies until the end of the operation.
- **On the map**: one tick per category, for example responders, external parties, casualties, PLS, search area, markers, areas, lines, helicopters, gauging stations, vehicles and the geodata layer. “Show all” and “Hide all” switch all at once.
- **Fixed locations** with their categories and “Names on the map”.

![“View” window](img/ansicht.jpg)

The selection only applies to the map on this device; the lists in the side windows remain complete. Hidden safety posts still limit the search area.

## Camera focus {#kamerafokus}

**Camera focus** aligns the map:

- **Search area**: the whole search area, with several PLS additionally per sub-area.
- **Responders**: all responders with location.
- **Auto overview**: at every update the map keeps all visible positions in view. It ends as soon as you pan or zoom the map by hand.

![Camera focus](img/kamerafokus.jpg)

In the list of responders you can **follow** a person: the map then stays on them. Following ends via ✕ in the notice at the top, when panning the map or when the person leaves the operation.

## Rotating the map {#drehen}

On a phone you rotate the map with two fingers, on a computer with Shift + mouse wheel or by dragging the compass above the zoom buttons. The compass needle always points north; a click on the compass aligns the map north again.

## Own location {#eigener-standort}

The blue dot with the accuracy circle is your own location, also without an operation. It is for display only. Your location is only sent when you [take part](#alarm/standort) in an operation. If you take part with a response option without location, the dot is hidden.

Without an operation the map shows your location and your organisation's fixed locations when opened.

## Search area {#suchgebiet}

The search area follows the river from the **PLS** (point last seen, the last known place of the missing person). Its length results from the flow speed and the time since the PLS. It covers the water and a bank strip of 30 m on each side and follows all branches at forks.

- A **safety or observation post** on the water ends the search area on its branch, as do **river barriers** such as weirs (see [River barriers](#leitung/flusssperren)).
- If each casualty has its own PLS, the search area is the union of the sub-areas.
- The notice at the bottom left summarises the search area; a click expands the details per PLS.
- If the river network is not available, the app draws a circle around the PLS.

All participants set the flow speed in the menu (card “Flow speed & search area”).
