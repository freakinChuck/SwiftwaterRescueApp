# Profile and settings

The 👤 button in the header of the menu opens your **profile** with all personal settings. “← Back” or the device's back button closes it.

![Profile](img/profil.jpg)

## Account {#konto}

Name, username and your response groups. Here you change your **profile picture** and your **password** and **log out**. Logging out deletes the state stored on the device. Under **Logged-in apps** you see the devices on which the native app is logged in with your account and can log them out.

## Alerting {#alarmierung}

- **Availability**: whether you are currently alarmed (see below).
- **Exercise mode**: for operation types that provide for it (for example exercises), you are alarmed as for a critical alarm.
- Switch **push notifications** on or off and send a test.
- **Install app**, if the browser offers it.
- **Test alarm**: choose the level (Critical, High, Normal, Silent) and tap “Test alarm in 30 s”. After 30 seconds an alarm of this level arrives for you only, without an operation. Meanwhile close the app or lock the screen to see whether sound, “Do not disturb” and push work as desired. “Cancel” stops the test beforehand.

> Without push on the device, the test alarm only arrives while the app is open. That is exactly what the test shows.

## Absence {#abwesenheit}

With **+ Record absence** you sign off for a period (holidays, illness) or suspend your availability. An empty “Until” means until further notice. During an absence:

- you are not alarmed (no push),
- you still see operations and can take part voluntarily,
- the menu shows the notice ⏸.

You end current absences with **End**, planned ones you remove. The administration can also record absences for you.

## Regular availability times {#einsatzzeiten}

Under **Alerting → Regular availability times** you define per weekday and time when you want to be alarmed. **+ Add rule** offers two kinds:

- **Regularly suspended**: during these times you are not alarmed, for example Mon–Fri 07:00–17:00 while at work.
- **On-call time**: as soon as you have at least one on-call time, you are only alarmed during your on-call times, for example on call Sat, Sun 00:00–00:00 (same time = whole day).

If “Until” is before “From”, the rule spans midnight; the hours after midnight count towards the chosen day (Fri 20:00–02:00 = Friday evening until Saturday 02:00). The rules work like an absence: no call-out alarm, running operations remain visible and you can join voluntarily. At the top, next to availability, you see whether you are currently “regularly suspended” or “outside on-call time” and from when you will be alarmed again. At most 20 rules per person; the administration can also record them for you.

## Calendar {#kalender}

The calendar (card **Calendar** in the menu without an operation, above the exercise schedule, in the profile next to the availability times, or in the header of the administration) shows everything with a date in one place:

- your absences and regular availability times,
- operations you took part in (with the right “Statistics” all of them),
- planned exercises and operations from the exercise schedule; if registration is open, you register or deregister directly there (**I will take part**, **I will not take part**, **Withdraw**). Exercise leaders see all plans with a date and open them in the administration with **Open**,
- training that expires.

At the top you change the month (‹ Today ›), switch between **Month** and **List** and show or hide categories. Tapping a day shows its entries. With the right “Manage absences” you choose “All persons” and see everyone's absences and availability times. The device remembers your choice.

## Display {#anzeige}

- **Language**: German, English, French or Italian. “Automatic” uses the device language, otherwise the organisation's default. The choice applies to your account (also in the administration and for push notifications); the page then reloads. In the administration you choose it in the account menu at the top right, at login below the “Log in” button.
- **Coordinate system**: LV95 (default), LV03, WGS84 decimal or in degrees/minutes/seconds, or hide coordinates.
- **Navigation app** for the 🧭 button: Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze or another app.

## Gauging stations {#messstationen}

In the **Water level & discharge** card you put together your own gauging stations (up to 20). Search by water body or place, ↑ moves, ✕ removes. They appear in the [Gauging stations](#einsatz/messstationen) window, also without an operation.

## Radio {#funk}

- **Listen in**: play new announcements immediately while the app is open.
- Operating the push-to-talk button: hold, or tap to start and stop.

## AI assistant {#ai}

For each function of the AI assistant you choose how far it supports you: off, **on demand** (button ✨) or **alongside** (the suggestion is ready when you open it). More than the administration allows is not possible.

## Vehicles {#fahrzeuge}

If vehicles are also tracked outside operations, you give your consent here that the native app reports beacons near you. **Scan vehicles now** immediately searches for vehicles near you and reports them (see [Vehicles and beacons](#app/beacons)). With the necessary right you register a new beacon here.

## Documents {#dokumente}

The **Documents** card shows how many entries are stored offline on the device and how many read confirmations are open. **Save offline now** updates the offline copy immediately, **My suggestions** shows your suggestions to the editors with their status. You can withdraw open suggestions. More under [Documents](#einsatz/dokumente).

## Training {#ausbildung}

The **Training** card shows your training and skills with validity: valid, expires soon, expired or missing (required). If allowed, you record a completion yourself. Outstanding items are also flagged by a notice in the menu.

## Version {#version}

The **Version** card shows the version of the app and of the server. **What's new** lists the changes of the latest versions. After an update this list appears once by itself.
