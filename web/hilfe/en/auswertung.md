# Evaluation

## My operations {#meine}

The 📋 button in the header of the menu opens **My operations**: all ended operations and exercises you took part in. At the top are your personal statistics (operations, operation time, response time, trips) with a filter by type and period.

![My operations](img/meine.jpg)

For each operation there is, as far as the administration allows:

- **Log**: the operation log,
- **Replay**: the recording on the map,
- **AAR**: the released after action review, as a view, Word or PDF.

Depending on the setting, other persons appear anonymously (“Responder 3”), you yourself always with your name. After anonymisation, log and replay are entirely anonymous.

## Operation log {#log}

The log shows the course with time and person. **Large mode** shows it full-screen and sorted by time. In the administration the **Anonymous** button hides the names. With the AI assistant there is a summary of the log.

## Replay {#replay}

The replay plays back a recorded operation on the map:

- **Time slider** and **Play** at 1 to 120 times speed; the space bar starts and stops, Escape closes.
- The bar below the slider shows the recorded periods; during pauses no persons are visible.
- **Trail length**: whole course, the last minutes or none.
- Next to it the situation, responders (with Centre and Follow) and the log up to the chosen time; a click on a log entry jumps there.
- The **View** window hides categories and changes the size of the positions; **Auto overview** keeps all positions in view.

## After action review {#aar}

An after action review (AAR) records the lessons learned from an ended operation. With the right “Create after action reviews” you open it in the administration under **Operation statistics and operation log** with **Record**:

- On the left the form with the organisation's fields (text, date, selection, images …), on the right operation data, feedback and log for reference.
- **Save** keeps the workspace open; ✨ suggests texts for empty fields with the AI assistant.
- **Save and release** (right “release”) completes the review. Participants see released reviews under “My operations”.
- **Not relevant** marks operations without a review.

Word and PDF are created in the browser. Before anonymisation the app secures released reviews as PDF.

## Operation feedback {#feedback}

After the end of the operation the lead can ask the participants for a short **feedback** (automatically if desired). Open requests appear as a notice in the menu and at the top of “My operations”. The answers flow into the after action review, anonymously if desired.

## Statistics {#statistik}

In the administration under **Evaluation** the right “Statistics” gives you:

- **Operation statistics and operation log**: key figures per operation type, list of all operations with log, replay and AAR.
- **Statistics per person**: operations, operation time, response time, trips and distance per person, with charts and CSV export.
