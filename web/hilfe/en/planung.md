# Exercises and planning

Besides spontaneous operations and exercises there are **prepared exercises**: the exercise lead defines in the administration what happens during the exercise and then releases the elements step by step. The same planning is available for planned secondary operations and free events.

## Preparing an exercise {#vorbereiten}

In the administration under **Planning** (right “Prepare exercises”):

1. **+ New exercise**: title, operation type, call-out message, exercise lead and call-out (response groups or individual persons).
   Under **Partner organisations** (only with an agreement for the operation type) you call out response groups of partner organisations and enter persons from there as exercise lead. At the start the partners get their own linked operation on their server; their exercise lead sees the exercise in their administration under “Planning” → “Joint exercises of partner organisations” (read only) and has the rights of the exercise lead in the linked operation.
2. Add **elements**, each with a location (coordinates or click on the map) and an optional message:
   - set or move the **PLS** of the operation,
   - **Casualty** with its own PLS,
   - **Sighting** of a casualty (new PLS),
   - make an **external responder** appear or disappear.
3. Optionally **automatic release** a certain number of minutes after the start.
4. **Map** shows a preview of all elements.

Fixed locations apply to the exercise as chosen; you add locations only for this exercise with “+ Location”. With **✨ Suggestions** the AI assistant suggests elements, if set up.

## Exercise schedule and registration {#uebungsplan}

In the exercise dialog, under **Exercise schedule and registration**, you set date, duration, place and information. The exercise then appears in the operations app, without an operation, in the **Exercise schedule** card. With registration switched on, persons register or deregister there until the registration deadline. If desired, those registered are called out at the start.

## Starting an exercise {#starten}

The exercise lead starts the exercise in the operations app: menu → **Planning** → choose exercise → **Start**. The persons called out are alarmed, not the exercise lead. The exercise lead has the rights of the operation lead and can also take part as a responder with **Also take part**.

## Releasing elements {#freigabe}

The **Exercise** tab of the menu lists all elements. Only the **release** makes an element visible to everyone; a PLS or casualty then counts for the search area, and the message goes to everyone as a message. When releasing you enter, if not prepared, the time of the PLS (empty = now, minutes or time of day). Automatic elements show “⏱ automatically at …” and can be released earlier.

## Runs {#durchfuehrungen}

Each run of a prepared exercise keeps its log and replay; you find them with the exercise in the administration. **Reset** (only ended ones) makes the exercise ready to start again, **Copy** creates a new one with the same elements.

## Secondary operations and free events {#sekundaer}

Operation types of the category **Secondary** are operations without alarm, for example a follow-up search the next day. They often refer to an ended operation and take over its PLS, flow speed and missing casualties. **Free events** have their own alarm level. Both can be planned like exercises, with an **operation plan** (time, meeting point, goal, tasks) that appears in the operation as the “Operation plan” card.
